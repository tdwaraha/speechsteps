Common questions

Is Speech Steps really free?

Yes. Every lesson and feature is included. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

How do I open Settings?

Tap the gear at the top of the home screen and answer the grown-ups question. This keeps children out of settings, deleting and sharing.

How do I change the voice?

Open Settings › Audio models. Choose a Voice and change the playback speed there.

For more natural voices, open the iPhone or iPad Settings app › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English, download an Enhanced or Premium voice, then choose it in Speech Steps.

My recording or camera isn't working
  • Check that access is allowed: iPhone or iPad Settings › Speech Steps › Microphone or Camera.
  • Close other apps that might be using the microphone or camera, then try again.
How do I make my child the star of the video lessons?

Press and hold your child's card on the home screen, choose Edit profile, then add a star photo. A clear, front-facing photo showing the whole head and a little of the shoulders works best.

Can I use Speech Steps with more than one child?

Yes, with as many children as you like. Each child has their own goals, progress and photos. Tap “Playing: … Change” on the home screen to switch, or use Play together for turn-taking games.

How do I share progress with our speech therapist?

Press and hold your child's card, choose Goals & progress, then tap Share PDF or Share CSV. You choose where the report goes.

How do I delete my child's information?

Open Settings (after the grown-ups check) › Privacy to delete all saved audio and videos, all real photos or all model videos. You can delete a goal from a child's goal list. To remove everything, delete the app. Because information is stored only on your device, we can't see it or delete it for you. See the privacy policy.

Does Speech Steps replace speech therapy?

No. Speech Steps is a practice and progress tool for families and professionals. It does not diagnose. If you have concerns about your child's speech or language, please talk to a speech-language pathologist.
