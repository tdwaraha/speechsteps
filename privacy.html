Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains how the Speech Steps app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”) and this website handle information. Speech Steps is made by {{DWARAHA T}} (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal information. We never see your child's name, photos, recordings or progress.
  • Everything you add to the app is saved only on your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

1. Information in the app

The app does not connect to our servers, and it does not have any. It works without an internet connection. Information you add is stored on your device so the app can work:

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Child profiles: name, birth year, avatar, notes for adults and comfort settingsTo personalise practice and keep each child's work separateOn your device
Goals, practice results, session notes, gems and rewardsTo track progress and create reportsOn your device
Star photo, real photos and pictures you addTo show your child in video lessons and use your own pictures for wordsOn your device
Audio recordings and model videos you makeTo play back practice attempts and modelsOn your device
Settings such as voice, speed and musicTo remember your choicesOn your device

We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports through the app. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code.

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's information. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

2. Permissions the app asks for

The app asks before using any of these, and you can change your answer at any time in iPhone or iPad Settings › Speech Steps. The app keeps working if you say no.

  • Microphone. To record optional speech attempts or adult models for playback. Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Camera. To take a star photo or photos of your own things, people and places, and for activities that use the camera. Photos and videos stay on your device.
  • Photos. The app cannot see your photo library. When you choose a photo, Apple's photo picker gives the app only that one photo.

3. Features provided by Apple

Some features use tools built into iOS and iPadOS. We do not receive any information from them.

  • Finding the face in a photo (to fit your child's star photo into video lessons) happens on the device with Apple's Vision framework.
  • Spoken words use the voices installed on your device.

4. When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it. For example, when you tap Share PDF, Share CSV or Print worksheet. You choose where it goes, such as an email to your child's therapist. Please check who you are sharing reports with, because they include your child's name and practice results.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Children's privacy

Speech Steps is designed for children to use with a parent, caregiver, teacher or therapist. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and the app has no way to send us information.

  • There are no ads, purchases or social features in the child areas of the app.
  • Settings, deleting data and sharing reports are behind a grown-ups-only check.
  • Children should not email us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We aim to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and the App Store rules for apps made for children.

6. Deleting information

  • In Settings (after the grown-ups-only check) › Privacy, you can delete all saved audio and videos, all real photos and all model videos.
  • You can delete a goal and its results from a child's goal list.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its information from the device.

Because we never receive app information, we cannot see or delete it for you. You are always in control of it on your device.

7. This website and email

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Like most hosts, GitHub's servers may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See GitHub's privacy statement. Text on the site uses fonts served by Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use it only to answer you and fix problems. We keep messages for up to 12 months after your question is resolved, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your message sooner.

8. Security

App information is protected by your device's own security, including your passcode and Apple's data protection. This website is served over HTTPS.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. The only information we could hold is a message you sent us. To make a request, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are in Canada, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how information is handled in the app, we will also describe it in the app's update notes.
