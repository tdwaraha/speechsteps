Speech practice that starts wherever your child is.

Speech Steps is a free iPhone and iPad app for parents and speech therapists. Children practise words, sentences and conversation with pictures, short video lessons where they are the star, and talk boards.

Download on the App Store See how it works

Free. No ads, no account.

From first words to conversation, one step at a time

The speech course follows a clear path. A grown-up picks the step that fits the child, and every lesson is always open, so you can go back or skip ahead any time.

  1. 1 Get ready Tap pictures and listen.
  2. 2 First words “pony”
  3. 3 Two words “pink pony”
  4. 4 All about me “My name is Leo.”
  5. 5 Full sentences “The pony can fly!”
  6. 6 Let's talk Taking turns in a chat.

Children can listen, watch and copy at their own pace, with calm, encouraging feedback.

What's inside

Everything is included in the free app.

  • 🎓

    Speech course

    11 steps from first words to conversation: understanding questions, two words together, all about me, full sentences, why and how, first-then-last stories, grammar and chatting.

  • 🎬

    Video lessons starring your child

    Add one photo and your child becomes the character who walks into the park, the beach or a party, then answers questions about what happens.

  • 🤝

    Talk situations and social talk

    48 role plays for real moments, like meeting a new friend or taking turns, plus a 30-day social talk program.

  • 🖼️

    Talk boards and sorting games

    Picture boards for naming, describing, actions, weather, the body and more. Tap to hear, then practise saying it. Sort-it games build categories and thinking words.

  • 💬

    Talk buttons

    An easy mode with tap-to-talk buttons, so children who use AAC can answer too.

  • 🗓️

    Visual schedule and calm settings

    Picture schedules, a calm mode with less movement, and a clean screen for children who are easily overwhelmed.

  • 👫

    Play together

    Brothers, sisters and friends take turns on one device and practise saying “Your turn!”

  • 📈

    Goals and progress reports

    Set goals for each child, score each try, and share a PDF or CSV report with your therapist.

Free, and your child's information stays on your device

Speech Steps has no subscription, no in-app purchases and nothing locked. It has no ads, no tracking and no sign-up.

Profiles, photos, recordings and progress are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Read the privacy policy for the details.

  • No account or email needed
  • No ads and no third-party tracking
  • Photos and recordings stay on the device
  • Reports leave the app only when you share them
  • Settings are behind a grown-ups-only check

Built for the grown-up sitting next to the child

Set goals for each child, then score each try. Speech Steps keeps track of progress for each goal.

Share progress as a PDF or CSV report with a speech-language pathologist, teacher or family member. Add your own pictures, recordings and model videos for the words that matter to your child.

Speech Steps is a practice and progress tool. It does not diagnose and does not replace assessment or therapy by a speech-language pathologist.
